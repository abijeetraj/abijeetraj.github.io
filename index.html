Invited Fellow
Johns Hopkins University

Hello! I'm Abijeet, a recent computer science graduate. I work on uncertainty in scientific machine learning: I want learned models of physical systems to report accurately how much they can be trusted, especially when they are rolled forward in time or used far from their training data.

I am currently an Invited Fellow in Prof. Somdatta Goswami's Centrum IntelliPhysics group at Johns Hopkins, where I work on computational bottlenecks in differentiable PDE solvers: writing custom sparse kernels and testing multigrid preconditioners and Krylov methods. Previously, I worked with Prof. Jinwoo An at the University of Texas Rio Grande Valley, building a computer-vision-to-PINN pipeline for fracture estimation and probabilistic surrogate models for materials data. During my undergrad at VIT, I interned remotely at Kyungpook National University under Prof. Bubryur Kim and Prof. Yuvaraj Natarajan, developing the vision and hazard-mapping models for an autonomous rover.

The common thread in my research is building AI that knows what it doesn't know. More broadly, I am interested in probabilistic machine learning and Bayesian inference. I am applying to PhD programs in ML and adjacent fields for Fall 2027.
